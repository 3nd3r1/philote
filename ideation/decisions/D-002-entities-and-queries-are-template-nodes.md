## Decision

Nodes come in three modes: **template** (deterministic codegen, no model), **model** (LLM backend) and **native** (handwritten, only checked). Entities and queries are template nodes.

## Why

Turning an entity into a TypeScript type, or a query into loader code, is mechanical. Sending it to a model adds cost and nondeterminism for nothing. Keeping the model for the parts that really are fuzzy makes builds cheaper and retargeting smaller.
